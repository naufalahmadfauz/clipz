## Question

How should complete evidence be transformed into a bounded, useful manual ChatGPT upload without silently equating acoustic salience with humor?

Decide whether to generate candidate regions, which nonsemantic signals may propose them, context padding, overlap merging, cross-track dialogue inclusion, deduplication, stable source IDs/times, and score explanation. Address deadpan jokes and funny conversations without loud events: provide an inspectable coverage strategy such as full transcript/chunk access instead of promising that event-rich selection is complete.

Define full versus compact output roles, budget controls and size estimation, multipart/chunk manifests, omitted coverage, links back to full evidence, and human rerun/reselection behavior. Do not make a fixed upload token limit a permanent schema guarantee.

Specify what manual analysis instructions or selection records make returned windows unambiguous and compatible with later marker export. Require a representative size/coverage trial, including the four-hour case, before treating the policy as validated.
